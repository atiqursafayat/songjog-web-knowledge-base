## Voice attributes

- Clear and accessible

- Respectful and human

- Community-centered

- Evidence-based

- Hopeful without exaggeration

- Specific about action and results

- Appropriate for communities, supporters, partners, volunteers, and donors

## Required terminology

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| projects | programme, pillar, front |
| focus areas | work fronts |
| participants or community members | the needy |
| descriptive link text | click here |
| supported result statements | transforming lives without evidence |

## Editorial principles

- Lead with why the activity matters, then explain what happened.

- Center participant agency and contribution.

- Use concrete verbs and short paragraphs.

- Distinguish activity, output, outcome, and impact.

- Use a proportionate CTA, not a donation ask by default.

## Heading rules

- Exactly one H1.

- Use H2, then H3, then H4 without skipping levels.

- Do not use H5 for styling.

- Prefer concise, natural, two-noun section headings where suitable.
