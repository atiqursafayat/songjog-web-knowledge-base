## Purpose

Record external media coverage about Songjog.

## Required inputs

Outlet; headline; publication date; source URL; summary; relevant Focus Area; approved image rights.

## Recommended structure

H1 coverage title; source and date; neutral summary; relevance to Songjog; external source link.

## Required handoff

Press Category; Focus Area; external URL; excerpt; SEO; media rights status.

## Agent checklist

- Use supported facts only.

- Ask blocking questions before finalizing.

- Provide human-readable draft and structured JSON.

- Default to Draft status.
