## Purpose

Create a short testimonial or direct quotation for dynamic quote sections.

## Required inputs

Exact wording; speaker-approved attribution; consent; source; related Post or Project; safety review.

## Recommended structure

Quote text; attribution; attribution detail; consent status; source reference; relation; Featured status.

## Required handoff

Quote Status; parent relationship; consent evidence; safe attribution; source location.

## Agent checklist

- Use supported facts only.

- Ask blocking questions before finalizing.

- Provide human-readable draft and structured JSON.

- Default to Draft status.
