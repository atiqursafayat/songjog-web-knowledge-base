## Purpose

Publish a Songjog-authored story, update, event, insight, or article.

## Required inputs

Category; responsible entity; date; location; related project; verified facts; quotes with consent; approved photos.

## Recommended structure

H1 title; standfirst; context and purpose; activity and participation; community perspectives; results and observations; what comes next; CTA.

## Required handoff

Category; Focus Area; tags; related project; SEO; quote objects; media plan; blockers.

## Agent checklist

- Use supported facts only.

- Ask blocking questions before finalizing.

- Provide human-readable draft and structured JSON.

- Default to Draft status.
