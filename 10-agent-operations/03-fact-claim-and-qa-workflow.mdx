## Claim transformations

| Source claim | Safe treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Workshop transformed careers | State the workshop occurred; claim outcome only with evidence. |
| Participants said they were empowered | Use exact approved quotes or describe recorded feedback accurately. |
| Hundreds benefited | Ask for the exact count and count definition. |
| Partnered with Organization X | Verify partnership approval and reporting period. |
