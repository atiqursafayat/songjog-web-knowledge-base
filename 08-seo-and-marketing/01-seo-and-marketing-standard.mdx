## Required SEO package

- Primary keyword

- Secondary keywords

- Search intent

- SEO title

- Meta description

- Suggested slug

- Excerpt

- Social title

- Social description

- Internal-link suggestions

## Guidelines

- Aim for an SEO title of about 60 characters or fewer.

- Aim for a meta description of about 150 characters.

- Use keywords naturally and avoid repetition.

- Make the title specific, accurate, and useful.

- Prefer descriptive internal-link anchors.

- Do not optimize unsupported claims.

## CTA guidance

- Match the CTA to the content purpose.

- Do not add a donation CTA automatically.

- Avoid urgency unless verified and necessary.

- Do not make financial-use claims without approved evidence.

## Internal-link priorities

- Related Project

- Relevant Focus Area

- Related Blog Posts

- Published Report

- Volunteer, partner, or donation page only when naturally relevant
