## Never transfer between entities

- Registration or legal status

- Tax deductibility or EIN

- Addresses and contact details

- Financial figures

- Project ownership

- Partnership claims

- Donation information

- Emergency operations or hotlines

## Required question

If the responsible entity is not explicit, ask: Which Songjog entity conducted or owns this activity?

## Handoff requirement

Every content package must contain entity.name and entity.verification_status.
