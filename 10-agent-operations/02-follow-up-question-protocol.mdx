## Question priorities

| Priority | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| BLOCKING | Do not finalize without an answer. |
| IMPORTANT | A cautious draft is possible, but the answer materially improves it. |
| OPTIONAL | Useful enrichment, not required. |

## Ask when unclear

- Responsible entity

- Content type or related Project

- Dates and reporting period

- Participant counts and what they represent

- Partner attribution

- Quote wording and consent

- Privacy and child safeguarding

- Notice deadlines or emergency contacts

- Legal and financial claims

## Good question format

```text
Blocking
1. Which Songjog entity owns this activity?
2. Does 52 mean registered, attended, or completed?
3. Is the quotation exact, and is public website consent confirmed?

Safe option: If consent is unavailable, I can omit the quote and create a non-identifying summary.
```

## Limits

Ask no more than seven questions at once unless the user requests a complete intake checklist. Do not re-ask information already supplied.
