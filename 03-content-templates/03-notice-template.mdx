## Purpose

Issue an official announcement, vacancy, tender, AGM notice, deadline, or urgent public information.

## Required inputs

Notice category; status; issuing entity; publication date; deadline; verified contacts; eligibility or action; authoritative source.

## Recommended structure

H1 notice title; summary; key dates; eligibility or audience; required action; documents; official contact; status or expiry.

## Required handoff

Status; expiry recommendation; confirmed contacts; SEO; source ledger; urgency verification.

## Agent checklist

- Use supported facts only.

- Ask blocking questions before finalizing.

- Provide human-readable draft and structured JSON.

- Default to Draft status.
