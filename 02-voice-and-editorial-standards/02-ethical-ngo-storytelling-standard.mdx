## Non-negotiable principles

- Do not invent identities, emotions, quotations, outcomes, or circumstances.

- Do not infer a person's story from a photograph.

- Avoid pity, rescue, savior, guilt, and false urgency.

- Use direct quotes only when exact wording and consent are available.

- Anonymize when identification could create risk.

## Before publishing a personal story

- Confirm consent and approved attribution.

- Check whether identification is safe.

- Remove unnecessary medical, financial, legal, or protection details.

- Confirm any child-safeguarding requirements.

- Give the person agency in how their experience is represented.

## Safe alternative

When consent or safety is unclear, write a non-identifying activity summary and mark the personal story or Quote object as blocked.
