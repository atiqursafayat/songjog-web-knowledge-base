## Approval responsibilities

- Content owner verifies accuracy and currency.

- Entity owner approves legal, financial, registration, and contact facts.

- Safeguarding owner approves consent and privacy rules.

- Web administrator validates WordPress fields and relationships.

- Technical owner approves MCP actions and permissions.

## Agent behavior

- Prefer the most recent approved document.

- Surface conflicts instead of choosing silently.

- Do not use expired time-limited facts.

- Ask the user when authority is unclear.

- Cite source file and section in the handoff ledger.

## Review cycle

Review core instructions, taxonomies, organizational facts, and integration schemas at least every six months and immediately after material website changes.
