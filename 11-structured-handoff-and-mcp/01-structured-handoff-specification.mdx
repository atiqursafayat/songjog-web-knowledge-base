## Readiness rule

ready_for_mcp must be false whenever entity, consent, privacy, required facts, required fields, or controlled taxonomy values remain unresolved.

## Content status

post_status must remain draft in routine agent output.

## Source traceability

Record source file and page, section, table, or user-provided clarification for dates, numbers, quotes, partners, outcomes, legal statements, financial statements, and emergency information.
